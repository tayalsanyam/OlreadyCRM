# MUA Partner Proposition

The MUA proposition must focus on business growth, verified queries, control, visibility, and support. A partner MUA should feel that Olready is helping them focus on makeup delivery while the platform supports discovery, enquiry flow, lead management, profile credibility, and follow-up structure.

## Merchant-side benefits

- Customer queries without depending only on the MUA’s own marketing.
- Ability to quote their own price and choose customers based on location, event date, budget preference, and service fit.
- Social media recognition through Olready channels where applicable.
- Business tools such as online payment support and GST invoicing support where enabled.
- Business profile management and review collection.
- Relationship-manager support for paid plan MUAs according to plan and internal workflow.
- A structured dashboard and account where the MUA can manage their profile and connect with potential clients.

## MUA partner flow

1. Sign up or enter the sales pipeline as a potential, existing no-plan, or lapsed MUA.
2. Salesperson explains the plan, shares details, schedules demo if needed, handles objections, and closes the deal.
3. Onboarding collects profile info, business info, plan details, social media, region/city, lead access criteria, and contract/invoice requirements.
4. Training explains profile link, lead unlock, lead budget check, lead reversal, RM role, RM contact, and lead views.
5. Activation verifies the profile, invoice reference, contract status, and plan fields before the MUA becomes active for RM servicing.
6. Active MUA is visible to RM portal and receives plan servicing according to plan tier, cap rules, region/city, and lead eligibility.

# Olready Assured - Trust Layer

Olready Assured is the trust and reliability layer for selected artists. It should be presented as a quality framework, not merely a badge. It gives customers confidence and gives MUAs a higher-trust positioning.

| Assured criterion | Meaning for customers and operations |
| --- | --- |
| Reputed and experienced professionals | Only professional artists meeting experience and reputation expectations should qualify. Experience, background, and customer reviews are part of the check. |
| Quality products used | Assured artists use high-end and international-quality makeup products. Public examples may include reputed global makeup brands, but avoid over-promising specific kit contents unless profile-verified. |
| Backup or full refund guarantee | If the artist cancels from their end, the assured promise is backup approved by the client or full refund according to applicable conditions. |
| Free telephonic consultation | Assured artists provide a free phone consultation after finalisation so the client can discuss skin concerns, preferences, and the desired look. |
| Trial availability | Assured artists provide makeup trial on request; trial terms differ by artist and must be profile-specific. |
| Profile completeness | Assured profiles should carry strong profile completion: travel, trial, payment policies, reviews, gallery, and relevant details to help customers decide. |

## How to use Assured in collateral

- Use “trusted makeup artists who follow Olready Assured standards” for public material.
- Use “backup support or refund assurance in case of artist-side cancellation, subject to policy and artist profile terms” for safer legal wording.
- Use Assured as a filter, badge, and confidence marker in future product design.
- Do not imply every MUA on Olready is automatically Assured.

# Plan Architecture - No Pricing Version

Olready paid plans define the access and servicing logic for MUAs. This source-of-truth version intentionally does not include pricing. The correct public-safe way to discuss plans is by outcome, exposure level, support level, and eligibility, not monetary value.

| Plan field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Plan name | The package/tier the MUA is subscribed to. Internal names include Privy/Highest Privy, Phoenix 2, Phoenix, Pro, and Prime. Final naming should follow admin configuration. |
| Validity | Plan starts and expires on defined dates. No extension should be assumed unless approved through policy. |
| Lead budget eligibility | Defines what budget category of leads the MUA is eligible to access or be suggested for. Exact monetary thresholds are excluded in this edition. |
| Access scope | Controls what type of leads, regions, cities, or dashboard access a plan includes. |
| Allocated leads / lead cap | Defines the overall lead access or unlock commitment where applicable. |
| Weekly profile push cap | Controls how many times an MUA profile can be pushed in a calendar week by plan tier. |
| RM support | Defines whether and how a Relationship Manager assists with follow-ups, suggestions, and conversion support. |
| Social media support | Defines whether the plan includes social recognition or promotional support. |
| Assured bookings | Used where a plan explicitly includes committed booking outcomes. Must be contract-controlled and carefully worded. |
| Olready Assist / commission model | Used where Olready earns commission per assisted booking or operates a commission lane. Terms should be plan-specific. |

## Plan tier exposure logic

| Plan tier | Exposure principle | Public-safe explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Privy / Highest Privy | Top exposure tier. | Best suited for MUAs who want maximum platform visibility and priority recommendation opportunity. |
| Phoenix 2 | High exposure tier. | Best suited for growth-focused MUAs wanting strong access and servicing. |

# Lead Reversal Policy - Master Summary

The lead reversal policy applies only to paid subscribers and only for leads added from plan activation to plan expiry. It exists to keep the platform fair, ensure issues are reported quickly, and allow Olready to verify bride status before issuing lead credit/replacement where eligible.

## Important rules

- Lead issues must be reported within the defined reporting window from unlock.
- No lead issue is entertained after the post-expiry reporting window.
- Olready may take up to the defined redressal time to verify the bride’s status and revert.
- MUA must make reasonable contact attempts at different times and on different days before calling a lead non-responsive.
- MUA should use calls, WhatsApp, and email where available because brides may not answer unknown numbers during wedding preparation.
- Do not repeatedly call the bride. Follow up respectfully on the next day by call or WhatsApp.
- Screenshots of WhatsApp conversations should be shared for cases such as no response, already booked, or changed requirements.

## Reporting channels

- Email care@olready.in.
- Report a Problem section in the Olready merchant dashboard.
- For eligible Select/assigned-manager partners, report to the assigned follow-up manager via WhatsApp.

## Eligible reversal conditions

- Client has booked someone else on the first attempt by call, WhatsApp, or email.
- Client says she was never looking for a makeup artist.
- Client is non-responsive to both MUA and Olready after repeated attempts over the defined verification period.
- Event location changes to another state.
- Event is cancelled.

# Sales CRM Operating System

## Purpose

The Sales CRM extends the RM platform but operates in its own sales namespace and schema. Its job is to move MUAs through a sales pipeline from first contact to active plan, then hand off cleanly to RM CRM. When a plan expires, Sales CRM picks the MUA back up for reactivation.

## Sales roles

| Role | Scope | Primary responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| salesRm | Own assigned pipeline. | Contacts MUAs, moves stages, logs calls and WhatsApps, closes deals. |
| salesTl | Own pipeline plus team visibility. | Works own pipeline, coaches team, owns Senior Call tasks, sees team analysis. |
| salesActivation | Activation queue. | Handles invoice reference, contract upload, final plan activation. |
| admin | Full sales access. | Assigns/reassigns MUAs, configures settings, manages imports, exports reports. |
| owner | Read-only analytics. | Monitors performance and strategy-level metrics. |

## Three MUA categories

| Category | Who they are | Where they live | CRM behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Active MUAs | MUAs currently on a live plan. | rm.muas | Managed by RM CRM; sales can read plan details and expiry. |
| Existing No Plan | MUA already known to Olready but currently without active plan. | rm.muas | Sales pipeline applies; prior RM history visible for context. |
| Potential MUAs | Prospects found from inbound, ads, referral, Instagram DM or other sources. | rm.muas with candidate status | Admin bulk upload; invisible to RM portal until activated. |

## Pipeline stages

The same pipeline applies to Existing No Plan and Potential MUAs. Every stage change is logged with actor, timestamp, prior stage, next touch point, and notes. Details Shared and Demo can occur in either order; the rest of the order is enforced.

| Stage | Task behavior | Important rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Untouched | Creates touch-point task. | Default entry state. |
| Not Connected | Creates callback task. | Use after attempted contact/no answer. |
| Call Back | Creates scheduled callback. | MUA asked for specific callback. |
| Follow Up | Creates touch point. | General follow-up stage. |
| Details Shared | Creates follow-up. | Pricing/plan overview shared; commercial amounts remain in separate controlled docs. |
| Demo Scheduled | Creates demo task. | Demo date/time stored. |
| Demo Done | Creates feedback call. | Demo completed; collect feedback. |
| Senior Call | Creates TL-only task. | Salesperson cannot close this stage; TL must action. |
| Confirm | Creates confirmation task. | Verbal confirmation received but not closed. |
| Deal Closed | Creates onboarding task. | Requires payment details in sales system; MUA exits active pipeline. |
| Rejected | No future task. | Requires rejection reason and optional note. |

## Task system

- salesFollowUp: standard follow-up task created at transitions.
- salesSeniorCall: TL-owned task; salesperson can see but not close.
- salesOnboarding: auto-created on Deal Closed for the salesperson.
- salesActivation: created after training completion for activation user.
- salesAssignRm: created when a lapsed MUA needs reassignment because original salesperson is inactive.

## Deal close and onboarding

- Deal Closed requires amount, date, payment mode, and optional notes in the CRM, but amounts are excluded from this document and public RAG.
- Checklist 1 captures profile info: MUA name, business name, official address, email, optional GST, alternate phone, and manager number.
- Checklist 2 captures plan details: plan, lead cap, lead budget eligibility, regions, cities, social media, duration, assured bookings if applicable, and average revenue target if defined.
- After onboarding checklists, training becomes available immediately.

## Training checklist

- Profile link entered and verified as a real URL.
- Lead unlock explained.
- Lead budget check explained.
- Lead reversal process explained.
- Role of RM explained.
- RM contact shared.
- Lead views shown to the MUA.

## Activation flow

1. Activation user verifies profile link.
2. Invoice is generated externally and reference number is captured.
3. Contract is generated and status becomes awaiting signature.
4. Signed contract is uploaded or linked.
5. Activate Plan button writes status, plan tier, expiry, regions, cities, social fields, lead cap, lead budget eligibility, and duration into rm.muas.
6. Admin is notified to assign RM after activation.

## Callyzer integration

- Each salesperson can have a linked Callyzer number stored on the user profile.
- Calls can be ingested through webhook and reconciled through scheduled polling.
- Calls are matched to pipeline records by phone number.
- Three or more outbound unanswered attempts should suggest Not Connected, but salesperson must confirm stage movement.
- Call entries appear in the MUA comms timeline alongside manual calls.

## AI sales assistant

The AI sales assistant is designed as an on-demand Chief Sales Officer in the salesperson’s pocket. It should not run background jobs or modify data. It helps draft WhatsApp messages, reply to objections, build personalized pitches, explain plans, answer product/process questions from uploaded knowledge-base documents, and plan the salesperson’s day from pipeline context.

- AI prompt should combine company sales persona, current MUA context, relevant knowledge-base documents, and the salesperson’s question.
- AI output is text-only and manually copied by salesperson.
- AI should follow the approved tone: professional but warm, confident not pushy, Hindi/English mix acceptable for WhatsApp where natural.

# Sales Team Operating Model

The sales team works the MUA side of the marketplace. The practical goal is not only to sell a plan but to move the right MUA into the right plan, activate them cleanly, train them properly, and set them up for RM-led servicing without gaps.

## Pipeline ownership

- Each salesperson owns assigned MUAs and must progress them through stages with logged next touch points.
- Team leads work their own pipeline and supervise their team pipeline.
- Senior Call is an escalation stage and should not be closed by the salesperson who raised it.
- Admin can reassign MUAs and manage unassigned records.

## Sales-sheet learnings to preserve

- RAW DATA should remain the source of truth; stage tabs or pipeline views should be filtered outputs, not separate truth copies.
- Stage movement must be dated and backed by communication notes or Callyzer activity.
- Refused, Not Connected, and Not Responding are not dead ends unless terminal rejection is explicitly logged with reason.
- Every non-connected MUA should have a scheduled callback or reattempt strategy, not open-ended follow-up.
- Hot pipeline stages such as Demo Done, Senior Call, Confirm, and Deal Closed require sharper daily ownership than early-stage leads.
- Pending tasks should be visible salesperson-wise and TL-wise so coaching is based on activity and movement, not only final closures.
- Callyzer should be used to verify attempt quality: call count, talk time, answer rate, and time-of-day effectiveness.

Olready helps professional makeup artists get discovered by high-intent customers and manage enquiries in a more structured way. Instead of depending only on random DMs or scattered referrals, you get a profile, verified customer opportunities, RM support where applicable, and a system designed to help you focus on your art while your business pipeline becomes more organized.

## After demo done

Hi [Name], thank you for taking the demo. Based on your work, city, and target customers, Olready can be a strong channel for structured makeup queries and business visibility. I suggest we close the plan details and move into onboarding so your profile and training can start.

## Objection: I already get leads from Instagram

That is great and it means your work is already creating demand. Olready should be seen as an additional structured channel, not a replacement for Instagram. Instagram brings attention; Olready helps organize verified customer intent, profile visibility, and support-led follow-up so you are not dependent on one source alone.

## Objection: I am not sure about ROI

That is a fair concern. The better way to evaluate Olready is to look at the quality of queries, your speed of follow-up, profile strength, and how consistently you engage with leads. We train you on the process, explain lead reversal, and support you through the RM system where applicable so the plan is used properly rather than passively.

## Objection: I want to try free

You can create visibility and understand the platform, but serious lead access and support are plan-led. The paid plan is meant for MUAs who want structured business opportunities, not just a listing. Let us first match the right plan to your city and work profile so you are not overcommitting or underusing the platform.

## Objection: I had a bad previous experience

I understand. Let us first look at what happened earlier: lead quality, response time, reversal requests, follow-up attempts, and RM support. The current process is more structured with clearer training, logged activity, lead reversal rules, and better handoff. We should restart only with clarity on how the plan will be used.

# Reputation and Trust Recovery - Internal Only

The strategic pivot document identifies reputation risk around the legacy perception of Olready as a database or lead seller. The internal response should be evidence-led, support-first, and operationally disciplined. Do not argue publicly. Improve the product, show clearer policies, train sales better, log activity, respond professionally, and create verifiable success stories.

- Shift language from “selling numbers” to “building MUA businesses through verified opportunities and systems.”
- Use beta/success stories and testimonials from properly serviced MUAs to rebuild trust.
- Sales should not overpromise lead volume, conversion, or bookings beyond plan terms.
- RM and Sales CRM should make every promise, follow-up, reversal, and issue traceable.
- Negative feedback should be routed to resolution and learning, not ignored.

> **Do not expose to public agents:** Reputation-risk notes are for leadership, operations, support, and internal training only. Public collateral should focus on trust, support, policy clarity, and success stories rather than discussing historical allegations.

# Competitive Playbook to Internalize

The competitor landscape suggests that the strongest beauty marketplaces combine consumer demand, booking convenience, pro-side software, trust badges, payments, and dynamic supply management. Olready should selectively adopt the ideas that fit Indian bridal makeup and MUA workflows.

| Play | What Olready can adapt |
| --- | --- |
| Assurance badge and SLA | Turn Olready Assured into a more formal guarantee with backup support, response expectations, kit standards, hygiene expectations, and profile completeness. |
| Instant book / faster quote loops | Reduce time from customer query to first artist response. |
| Deposits and no-show protection | Use payment/deposit rails where appropriate to reduce cancellation friction and increase seriousness. |
| Smart availability and last-minute fill | Highlight artists available for urgent or near-date events. |
| Paid exposure with fairness | Use capped, transparent exposure logic rather than letting only the strongest MUAs absorb all attention. |
| Content-commerce flywheel | Create bridal inspiration, look guides, city/service pages, and instantly actionable artist recommendations. |
| Partnership channels | Explore hotels, wedding planners, venues, photographers, and luxury stays as demand partners. |
| MUA SaaS lock-in | Profiles, CRM, reviews, payment support, quote management, and follow-up systems make the platform harder to leave. |
