# Executive Company Narrative

Olready is a beauty-tech platform operated by KSM Beauty Pvt. Ltd. The brand promise is to make beauty discovery, makeup consultation, and MUA business growth more structured, trustworthy, and conversion-oriented. The company connects brides and other beauty customers with makeup artists, while also serving MUAs as paid business partners who need verified demand, RM support, digital profile infrastructure, social recognition, reviews, structured follow-up, and a stronger business operating system.

The company should be described as a two-sided beauty marketplace with a strong B2B backend. The bride side creates high-intent makeup queries. The MUA side receives access to verified opportunities, profile exposure, platform tools, and relationship-manager support according to plan rules and internal allocation logic. Internally, Olready is moving toward a more system-led model: leads as perishable stock, RMs as conversion operators, salespeople as MUA acquisition and reactivation specialists, and the platform as a ledger-driven operating layer for makeup commerce.

The long-term strategic direction is to move away from the perception of being only a database or lead seller. The preferred positioning is: Olready helps beauty professionals build, manage, and grow their makeup business through verified demand, structured CRM support, assured trust systems, and eventually deeper growth tools.

# What Olready Does - Public and Backend View

Olready helps users discover salons and makeup artists under one roof. ## MUA-facing backend view

For MUAs, Olready is not just a listing page. It is a business-support platform. The merchant proposition includes customer leads, own pricing/quotation control, ability to choose customers by location/date/budget preference, social media recognition, online payment and GST invoicing tools, profile management, and reviews. The operational flow on the MUA side is: get requests, unlock preferred requests, and connect with customers by call or mail to get hired.

## Internal operating view

Internally, Olready works through demand generation, lead verification, regional RM allocation, plan-based MUA suggestion, parallel MUA conversations, booking confirmation, issue handling, and commission handoff for aged or non-plan opportunities. The internal CRM should treat every lead and every MUA interaction as a ledger entry: actor-stamped, chronological, immutable, and available to the next operator in the lifecycle.

# Lead Operating Model

Olready should treat leads as perishable stock. A lead loses value as the event date approaches, as the bride becomes non-responsive, or as a competitor books her first. The CRM therefore sorts, escalates, and logs leads through urgency, budget category, region, and assignment windows.

## Lead lifecycle

1. Lead source generates raw enquiry: Instagram, campaign, referral, Karwan or other inbound source.
2. Lead Uploader verifies core details: name, phone, email, event date, city, budget preference, source, and completeness.
3. Admin or Owner assigns lead to Regional RM based on region and operational need.
4. Lead enters RM queue and is auto-sorted by urgency, budget tier, and days to event.
5. RM opens the lead profile, suggests eligible Plan MUAs, and logs all communication and stage movement.
6. Multiple MUA conversations may run in parallel within per-lead cap rules.
7. Booking can be confirmed event-by-event for multi-event weddings.
8. If not converted within the assignment window, or if the bride does not want plan-network MUAs, the lead can move to Commission RM lane.
9. If hostile or invalid under policy, the lead can be archived with ledger notes and admin visibility.

## Lead budget tiering

| Tier | Operational meaning | Use in RAG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | Highest-value lead category with strongest priority push. | Describe as premium/high-budget lead without monetary thresholds. |
| Tier 2 | Strong-value lead category with high conversion focus. | Describe as premium-mid category without thresholds. |
| Tier 3 | Large-volume standard lead category. | Describe as standard-volume lead category. |
| Tier 4 | Lower budget/high-volume category, often suitable for commission or broader pool handling. | Describe as entry/broad category without thresholds. |

## Lead urgency bands

| Band | Meaning | Operational behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Event is very close. | Always top priority; cap bypass may be allowed with logged reason. |
| Hot | Event is approaching soon. | High priority; inactivity must be watched closely. |
| Active | Event has workable lead time. | Normal cap and queue rules. |
| Long Shelf | Event is far enough away. | Lower queue priority; caps strictly enforced. |

# RM Platform Operating System

## RM roles

| Role | Scope | Primary responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regional RM | Assigned region only. | Works Plan MUA leads, suggests profiles, logs communications, drives booking. |
| Commission RM | All regions. | Picks up aged-out leads or leads outside Plan MUA lane and works from broader MUA pool. |
| Lead Uploader | Upload/verification queue only. | Imports, verifies, deduplicates, and prepares leads before RM assignment. |
| Admin | Full operational access. | Configures settings, assigns leads, manages plans, caps, reports, and overrides. |
| Owner | Full visibility and override. | Platform analytics, strategic decisions, and final configuration authority. |

## RM queue design

- Region tabs: North, East, West, South.
- Each RM sees only the leads assigned to their region unless their role allows broader access.
- Queue is auto-sorted by urgency band, then budget tier, then days to event.
- Each lead card shows bride name, lead ID, city, region, event location, budget tier, urgency band, days to event, assigned date, assignment-window countdown, number of MUA profiles suggested, last activity date, and multi-event indicator.
- Every lead has two clocks: event urgency and assignment window. One drives priority; the other drives commission shift risk.

## Lead profile screen

- Lead details header: bride contact, city, region, event location, budget, source, urgency, assignment countdown, verification status, and event summary.
- Parallel MUA conversations: each suggested MUA has plan tier, quote, stage, days since update, active/inactive flag, and events covered.
- Conversation stages: Initial Contact, Offer Sent, Follow-Up Done, Negotiating, Bride Selected.
- Per-lead cap prevents overwhelming the bride and keeps recommendations curated.
- Exit statuses include Not Interested in Plan MUAs and Hostile, each with different downstream handling.

## Lead ledger principle

The ledger is the single source of truth. Every creation, verification, assignment, push, stage update, cap bypass, call, WhatsApp, handoff, close-for-confirmation, booking, hostile flag, and expiry check-in should be actor-stamped and append-only. Corrections are new entries; nothing should be deleted.

## Cap engine

- Per-lead cap controls how many active MUA profiles can be in conversation for one bride at once.
- Per-MUA weekly cap controls how many different leads an MUA profile can be pushed to in a week based on plan tier.
- Critical urgency can permit cap bypass with a mandatory reason and admin-visible ledger entry.
- Closing unselected MUA conversations frees cap capacity where applicable.

## Commission RM lane

- A lead shifts after the defined assignment window if no booking is confirmed.
- A Regional RM can also shift a lead immediately by marking Not Interested in Plan MUAs.
- Commission RMs see full prior Regional RM ledger history in read-only mode.
- Non-plan MUAs have no weekly plan cap in the commission lane; plan MUAs still follow standard tier caps.

## Multi-event booking logic

- Indian weddings often include Haldi, Mehndi, Sangeet, Wedding, Reception, and custom ceremonies.
- A lead can contain multiple events, each with its own date, booking status, assigned MUA, and quote.
- One MUA can be proposed for multiple events on the same lead and should count once toward the per-lead cap.
- Bookings can be confirmed event by event. The lead fully closes only when all required events are booked or marked not needed.
- Group bookings are captured as notes in the current logic and should be structured in a future increment.

## RM activity reports

- Lead-level activity: all ledger entries, inactivity gaps, days since last touch.
- RM-level portfolio: queue by urgency, critical/hot inactivity, cap bypasses, conversion by tier, commission shifts.
- Platform analytics: lead volume, conversion by tier/region/RM, loss attribution, expired tiers, MUA utilization.
- AI weekly digest is a deferred add-on after core reports stabilize.

# Lead Reversal Policy - Master Summary

The lead reversal policy applies only to paid subscribers and only for leads added from plan activation to plan expiry. It exists to keep the platform fair, ensure issues are reported quickly, and allow Olready to verify bride status before issuing lead credit/replacement where eligible.

## Important rules

- Lead issues must be reported within the defined reporting window from unlock.
- No lead issue is entertained after the post-expiry reporting window.
- Olready may take up to the defined redressal time to verify the bride’s status and revert.
- MUA must make reasonable contact attempts at different times and on different days before calling a lead non-responsive.
- MUA should use calls, WhatsApp, and email where available because brides may not answer unknown numbers during wedding preparation.
- Do not repeatedly call the bride. Follow up respectfully on the next day by call or WhatsApp.
- Screenshots of WhatsApp conversations should be shared for cases such as no response, already booked, or changed requirements.

## Reporting channels

- Email care@olready.in.
- Report a Problem section in the Olready merchant dashboard.
- For eligible Select/assigned-manager partners, report to the assigned follow-up manager via WhatsApp.

## Eligible reversal conditions

- Client has booked someone else on the first attempt by call, WhatsApp, or email.
- Client says she was never looking for a makeup artist.
- Client is non-responsive to both MUA and Olready after repeated attempts over the defined verification period.
- Event location changes to another state.
- Event is cancelled.

# Plan Contract - Master Legal Summary

The plan contract should be understood as the legal control document for paid MUA relationships. It defines the plan, validity, access, lead budget eligibility, allocated leads, RM support, social media support, booking commission terms, payment principle, lead usage policy, reversal policy, non-guarantee, confidentiality, conduct, disputes, jurisdiction, force majeure, and indemnification. This master dump does not include bank details, tax identifiers, or payment amounts.

## Commercial/legal principles

- Plan cost is non-refundable and non-transferable once payment is processed and the plan is activated.
- Olready provides verified leads/queries and platform support, but the MUA remains responsible for initiating contact and converting leads into bookings.
- Where applicable, RM assists with follow-ups, but no specific conversion rate should be guaranteed unless contractually committed.
