# Olready Bride Side Knowledge Bank

## 1. Purpose

This knowledge bank is the public-safe source of truth for bride/customer-facing communication, WhatsApp scripts, chatbot answers, website FAQ, support replies and brochure copy.

The bride-side tone must feel like a premium beauty concierge. Olready should not sound like a listing marketplace, a lead seller or a sales agent. Olready should sound like a calm, experienced guide helping a bride make one of the most personal decisions for her big day.

## 2. Core Emotional Truth

A bride is not just looking for makeup. She is trying to protect her confidence, her pictures, her family expectations and the memory of one of the most important days of her life.

Use language that recognizes emotional pressure without making her anxious.

Approved emotional line:

> We know this isn't just makeup. It's your day, your pictures, your memories, and your confidence.

## 3. Bride-Side Positioning

Olready is your personal makeup concierge for bridal and event makeup. We help you share your requirement, discover relevant makeup artists, compare suitable profiles, connect with artists you like and receive support when you need guidance.

## 4. Approved One-Liners

- Your makeup artist search, handled with care.
- For your big day, you deserve more than options. You deserve guidance.
- You focus on your big day. We'll help you find the right artist.
- From confusion to confidence - Olready helps you choose better.
- Trusted makeup artist suggestions, made personal.
- We are not here to overwhelm you with choices. We are here to help you find the right fit.

## 5. What Olready Does for Brides

Olready helps brides and event customers:

- Share makeup requirements in a simple way.
- Get relevant makeup artist suggestions.
- Explore artists for bridal, engagement, family, party and destination events.
- Compare profiles, experience and style.
- Speak to artists before booking.
- Ask for more options if needed.
- Stay connected with Team Olready for guidance.

## 6. What Olready Does Not Do

Use this carefully in chatbot and support replies:

- Olready is not the final makeup service provider unless specifically stated in writing.
- Olready is not a salon or makeup artist.
- Olready does not decide the artist on behalf of the bride.
- Olready does not guarantee the final makeup result, because makeup is personal and depends on the selected artist, discussion, skin, products, style and event conditions.
- Olready does not override the individual cancellation, trial or payment terms agreed between the bride and the artist.

## 7. Approved Bride Journey

### Step 1: Share your requirement
Ask for event type, date, city/location, budget comfort, style preference and number of people.

### Step 2: We understand your occasion
Understand if the need is bridal, roka, engagement, haldi, mehndi, sangeet, cocktail, wedding, reception, family makeup, party makeup or destination wedding.

### Step 3: We suggest relevant artists
Suggest artists that fit the event, location, budget comfort, style and availability.

### Step 4: You compare and connect
The bride should review artist profiles, work, experience, inclusions and pricing before choosing.

### Step 5: We stay available
Team Olready stays available for more options, doubts, coordination support and guidance.

## 8. Lead Intake Fields

Capture only what is naturally needed. Do not interrogate the bride.

- Name

- Event city/location
- Budget comfort

## 9. Conversational Intake Rules

- Start warm and reassuring.
- Do not force a rigid form.
- Use: "Can we just confirm a couple of things so we can recommend the right artists?"
- Ask for date only if relevant or available.
- Ask budget comfort naturally.
- If the bride is unsure, guide her rather than pressuring her.
- End with a clear next step.

## 12. Chatbot Answers - Short Public Responses

### What is Olready?
Olready is a beauty-booking and makeup concierge platform that helps brides and event customers discover and connect with trusted makeup artists for bridal, family, party and destination events.

### Is Olready free for brides?
The initial requirement understanding and artist suggestion process is free for brides/customers. Artist charges, trials, travel, extra members and final service terms are discussed directly with the artist.

### Are artists verified?
Olready works with professional makeup artists and aims to suggest relevant and reliable profiles. Some artists may have enhanced trust checks or Olready Assured status. We still recommend reviewing the artist's work and confirming all details before booking.

### What is Olready Assured?
Olready Assured is a trust layer for selected artists who meet higher standards around experience, profile completeness, reviews and professionalism. Not every artist on Olready is automatically Olready Assured.

### Can I get more options?
Yes. If the first set of profiles does not match your style, budget or comfort, Team Olready can help you explore more suitable options.

### Who takes payment?
In most cases, final pricing and payment terms are discussed directly between the customer and the artist. Pay only after confirming all booking details in writing. For any official Olready payment, verify it with Team Olready first.

### What if an artist cancels?
Inform Team Olready immediately. We will try to help with alternate artist suggestions based on date, city, availability and urgency. Any specific assurance or refund depends on the booking terms shared at confirmation.

### Can you help with destination weddings?
Yes. Share the destination, dates, functions and whether you prefer a local artist or a travelling artist. We will try to suggest relevant options.

## 13. Safety and Confirmation Guidance

Before booking, ask the bride to confirm:

- Artist name and business name
- Event date and timing
- Venue/location
- Services included
- Hairstyling and draping inclusions
- Number of people covered
- Trial terms
- Travel and early morning charges
- Advance amount and balance payment
- Cancellation/refund policy
- Backup terms, if any
- Event-day arrival time and contact person

## 14. Escalation Rules

Escalate internally when:

- Event is within 7 days.
- Artist has cancelled after confirmation.
- Bride is distressed or angry.
- Payment confusion involves Olready's name.
- Bride claims misrepresentation.
- Multiple artists have called and bride is overwhelmed.
- Destination wedding or multi-event wedding needs urgent coordination.
- Any privacy or safety concern is raised.

## 15. Public-Safe Policy Notes

- Do not expose internal lead caps, MUA plans, plan tiers, sales stages, CRM logic or commission logic to brides.
- Do not call the bride a lead in public communication.
- Do not guarantee artist availability unless confirmed.
- Do not guarantee makeup outcome.
- Do not promise refunds unless written terms confirm it.
- Always keep the tone calm, helpful and bride-first.
